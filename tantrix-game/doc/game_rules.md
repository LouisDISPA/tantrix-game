# Tantrix Game Rules

## Overview

2-4 players. 56 unique hexagonal tiles with colored links. Each player picks a color (red, yellow, green, blue). Goal: build the longest line or loop in your color.

## Setup

1. All 56 tiles are shuffled into the bag.
2. The turn order is random (the printed rules let the highest drawn tile
   start).
3. In turn order, each player draws 6 tiles, placed face-up and visible to all.

## Golden Rule

All touching tile edges must match in color. Always.

## Turn Structure

Each turn has 3 steps:

1. **Fill forced spaces** you can.
2. **Make one free move** (play any tile anywhere legal).
3. **Fill forced spaces** again.

After each tile played (forced or free), draw a replacement while the bag has tiles. Keep a hand of 6 until the bag empties.

## Forced Spaces

A forced space is any empty hex surrounded by 3+ tiles. If a player has a tile that fits, they **must** play it. Filling one forced space may create another, which must also be filled immediately if possible.

## Three Restriction Rules (while bag has tiles)

These restrictions apply **only while tiles remain in the bag**. All three are lifted the moment the last tile is drawn.

1. **No same-color triple**: Cannot create a forced space where all 3 surrounding links are the same color (no tile could ever fit). Also, if filling a forced space would create an adjacent forced space with 3 same-color links, the original must not be filled.
2. **No 4-sided spaces**: Cannot surround a forced space with a 4th tile (creating a space bounded by 4 tiles). No tile may go next to a forced space, not even one filling another forced space beside it.
3. **No playing along controlled sides**: The straight lines of empty spaces that run outward from a forced space along the board's edge are its "controlled sides". No tile may go on a controlled side while the forced space is open.

## Endgame

When the bag is empty, the 3 restriction rules are lifted immediately. Forced spaces must still be filled when possible, before and after the one free move. Touching colors must still match. No replacement tiles are drawn once the bag is empty.

Source: [Official Tantrix Game Rules — The Endgame](https://www.tantrix.com/english/TantrixGameRules.html).

## Scoring

A player who cannot play passes. The game ends when every tile has been played, or when tiles remain but none fits anywhere. The app also lets players end a game early; the board is scored as it stands.

- **Line**: 1 point per tile in your longest continuous line of your color.
- **Loop**: 2 points per tile in your longest closed loop of your color.
- Only a player's single best score (line or loop) counts.
- Highest score wins; tied players share the win.

## FAQ Summary

- You may play tiles without your own color on them.
- Tiles only need to touch 1 existing tile (touching 2+ is a Gobble variant rule, not standard).
- You always get exactly one free move per turn, even if you filled zero forced spaces.
- You may count tiles remaining in the bag at any time (but not look at them).
- If a player forms multiple loops, only their best single score counts.
- The game does not end when one player runs out of tiles; play goes on while anyone can play.
